What do we look for?

If you are passionate about development, then it is time to work on a great Open Source project where your work will count! 

With emerging technologies and trends, offshore development services are becoming more crucial as they can do a galaxy of work for various industries like SaaS, E-commerce, Internet and IT.

Odoo is looking for experienced and driven Offshore Software Developers who can with their expertise in Git, JavaScript, SQL, HTML, CSS, PHP, Python, strategize, plan, execute and support significant growth for the comprehensive development of the company. As a developer, you will be responsible for designing, reviewing, and testing code within the Odoo ecosystem. 

Join our experienced team of Developers, and develop things people care about. Each employee has a chance to see the impact of his work and make a real contribution to the success of the company.

  • Programming Languages: Python and JavaScript
  • Database: PostgreSQL (With Object Rational Mapping)
  • Collaboration Platform: GitHub
  • Development Model: Open with external community
  • Framework: Odoo (CRM, Workflows, Report Engine, BI, AI)

Product Complexity

Responsibilities
  • Design and develop integrated Odoo application using proper convention and design standards
  • Analyze, diagnose and resolve errors related to the applications
  • Develop solutions for our clients from many different industries, sectors and company size
  • Provide support to maintain specific solutions
Must Have
  • Bachelor or Master Degree 
  • Odoo framework knowledge
  • Minimum 1 years of experience as Odoo Developer
  • Object-oriented programming, object modeling and relational database
  • Passion for development
  • Quick & Autonomous learner
Nice to Have
  • Contribution to Open Source Projects
  • Interest in enterprise application development
  • Techno-functional expertise
  • Linux, GitHub
  • Read & Write English
