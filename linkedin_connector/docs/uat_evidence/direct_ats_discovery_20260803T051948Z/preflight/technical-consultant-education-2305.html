Technical Consultant - Education

Marketing / Education
501, Building A1-A2, Dubai South Business Park, Dubai DU, United Arab Emirates
Apply

We are seeking a technical development specialist to join our Education Department as a Technical Consultant to support the expansion and implementation of our Odoo Education Program across the Pakistan, Turkey and Central Asian region.


The Odoo Education Program connects academic theory from computer science courses with real-world business applications by integrating Odoo’s Open Source Code into the classroom.


As a Technical Education Consultant, your mission is to advocate for the adoption and use of our Open Source Code in universities and training centers, empowering future computer engineers to gain hands-on experience and develop practical, industry-relevant skills.


Your role will not only focus on building and managing strong partnerships with educational institutions, but also on delivering these technical workshops to students, advising faculty on best practices, and supporting a seamless integration of Odoo into academic curriculum. 


Responsibilities

Work with professors, instructors, and administrators to introduce and implement Odoo ERP within business and technical courses.

Provide technical consultation and tailored guidance on how to effectively integrate Odoo into diverse classroom settings.

Conduct hands-on workshops and training sessions for both educators and students, ensuring technical comprehension and engagement.

Develop and support curriculum content that aligns with ERP, business, and IT educational goals.

Act as a liaison between the education sector and the Odoo to ensure smooth deployment and troubleshooting of the software for educational purposes.

Organize and manage educational events, including webinars, on-site demos, and competitions that promote the program and build student competency with Odoo.

Monitor and assess program usage to identify areas for improvement and additional support.

Qualifications

Background in computer science; experience with ERP systems is a plus.


Strong communication skills with the ability to translate technical concepts for non-technical audiences.


Comfortable delivering presentations and training sessions to varied audiences.


Project management skills to handle multiple educational partnerships simultaneously.


Passion for consulting and technology.

0-1 year of experience.

Must Have

Experience with programming languages (incl. object-oriented), specifically strong knowledge on Python framework.


Experience with relational databases.


Problem-solving skills.


Professional proficiency in English and Russian or Turkish or Urdu language.

What's great in the job?

Get ready for some serious responsibilities and the freedom to shine in a rapidly evolving company. You'll be part of an awesome team in a friendly and open work culture.

Expect diversity in your projects, giving you a chance to dive into various business industries and broaden your horizons. And here's the cherry on top: you'll get two weeks of intensive functional training, culminating in a shiny certification.
